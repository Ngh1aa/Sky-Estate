# Reference Analysis — KHOẢNG

This document captures reference intelligence for product/design decisions. It is not a layout-copying guide.

## 1. The Modern House

### Positioning
Design-led estate agency focused on thoughtful living and homes with strong attention to architecture, light, materials and nature.

### Useful patterns
- Property discovery is supported by strong editorial identity.
- Listings are treated as design objects, not only inventory.
- Photography carries substantial hierarchy.
- Search is expected to be fast, contextual and tolerant of imperfect input.
- Editorial and listing content can cross-pollinate discovery.

### Design implications for KHOẢNG
ADOPT:
- living-quality metadata;
- editorial depth inside residence detail;
- quiet photography-first pacing.

ADAPT:
- translate modernist design principles to contemporary Vietnamese urban/lifestyle housing.

REJECT:
- copying its exact monochrome/minimal structure, navigation, or card treatment.

Sources:
- https://themodernhouse.com/about/
- https://themodernhouse.com/journal/finding-the-right-home-faster

## 2. Inigo

### Positioning
Historic-home estate agency built around authentic details, provenance and compelling stories.

### Useful patterns
- Historic context is treated as part of product value.
- Property pages and editorial storytelling support each other.
- Emotional character is not hidden behind specifications.

### Design implications for KHOẢNG
ADOPT:
- story as decision context;
- material/character tags where supported.

ADAPT:
- use a contemporary, restrained system rather than heritage ornament.

REJECT:
- vintage decorative styling, heritage palette or copy tone as a direct model.

Source:
- https://www.inigo.com/

---

## 3. JamesEdition

### Positioning
Large-scale global luxury marketplace with strong conventional search and personalisation tools.

### Useful patterns
- Familiar search inputs remain essential for serious property browsing.
- Custom filters, wish lists, alerts and personalised feeds make large inventories manageable.
- Conversion remains a direct contact with listing owner/agent.

### Design implications for KHOẢNG
ADOPT:
- conventional filters as a secondary control layer;
- shortlist/saved-state mental model;
- clear consultation/contact action.

ADAPT:
- reduce density because KHOẢNG is a curated prototype, not a massive global marketplace.

REJECT:
- global marketplace scale language and dense portal aesthetics.

Source:
- https://www.jamesedition.com/real_estate

## 4. Sotheby's International Realty

### Positioning
Luxury property network with a strong editorial layer across architecture, destination and lifestyle.

### Useful patterns
- High-value homes are described through relationship to place and lived experience.
- Architecture, privacy, craftsmanship, views and landscape are treated as meaningful differentiators.
- Editorial storytelling extends beyond transactional listing content.

### Design implications for KHOẢNG
ADOPT:
- connect architectural qualities to daily use;
- treat place and landscape as part of residence identity.

ADAPT:
- make the language more accessible and less status-driven.

REJECT:
- borrowed luxury symbolism, prestige claims and auction-house associations.

Sources:
- https://www.sothebysrealty.com/extraordinary-living-blog/new-and-notable-properties-september-2026/
- https://www.sothebysrealty.com/extraordinary-living-blog/new-and-notable-properties-june-2026/

## 5. Knight Frank — Residence Report 2026/27

### Current market intelligence used
- Luxury residential value is increasingly described through location, authenticity, belonging, exceptional experiences and wellness rather than only amenity quantity.
- Nature, lifestyle destinations and ease of living have become more prominent themes.
- Place, provenance, judgement and human connection are framed as difficult-to-replicate sources of differentiation.

### Design implications for KHOẢNG
ADOPT:
- living qualities should include environmental and experiential characteristics, not only premium amenities;
- avoid equating luxury with more badges/features.

ADAPT:
- KHOẢNG is not positioned as a UHNW/branded-residence service, so these insights inform design direction only.

REJECT:
- any attempt to imply Knight Frank market findings prove KHOẢNG's concept or user demand in Vietnam.

Sources:
- https://www.knightfrank.com/research/reports/global/the-residence-report
- https://www.knightfrank.com/research/article/2026/9/residence-report-2026-new-scarcity-luxury-developments
- https://www.knightfrank.com/research/article/2026/9/residence-report-2026-wellness-branded-residences

# Cross-reference synthesis

## Common evidence-backed principles

1. Premium property products benefit from strong photography, but photography alone is not a product strategy.
2. Architecture, setting and lived quality can be decision metadata, not merely editorial decoration.
3. Conventional search/filter controls remain necessary even when discovery is differentiated.
4. Editorial context can help a user understand why a home matters.
5. Premium positioning is stronger when it comes from judgement, curation and information confidence rather than exaggerated visual effects.

## KHOẢNG-specific synthesis

The product should combine:

`FAMILIAR PROPERTY FACTS`
+
`LIVING QUALITY INDEX`
+
`EDITORIAL RESIDENCE STORY`
+
`CLEAR CONSULTATION / SHORTLIST ACTION`

The new experience must not become a clone of any single reference.
